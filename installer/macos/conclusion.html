Ready to go

Open AI Usage Widget from the Applications folder in your home folder.

Use the menu bar icon to select your providers and layout. Enable Start at login if you want the widget to appear automatically.

Codex and Grok require their respective CLI logins. For Grok Bot, sign in to its desktop app, enable Subscriptions → Grok Bot in the widget, and allow access to Grok Bot Safe Storage if Keychain asks.
